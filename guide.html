3
Capture your first contact

Two modes — pick the one that fits the moment.

⚡ Quick Capture — Header Button — 10 Seconds
Tap Quick Capture at the top of the app — always visible. Name and phone or email. Save. Done. Complete their profile later by tapping Edit.
⚡ Hot Intro — Full Capture
Tap Hot Intro in Quick Actions. Fill in all details. Upload a photo of their business card from Gallery and AI fills the form automatically.
📱 Scanning Tip
Always take photos with your regular camera app first — saves to your camera roll automatically. Then tap Upload from Gallery in FollowOp. Your photo is always safe even if you close the app mid-capture.

4

Conversations, contacts captured, intros made, social posts. ROI score calculated automatically.
5
Read The Room — before you follow up

Before you call or email anyone — Read The Room. Take a photo of them, upload their LinkedIn screenshot, paste their URL — or use their business card. AI gives you their communication style and a ready-to-send follow-up message.

📸 Photo of them — reads their energy, style, and personal presence
🪪 Business card photo — reads their professional presence and extracts contact info
📱 LinkedIn or social screenshot — most complete picture of who they are
🌐 Website or bio screenshot — reads how they present their brand
📝 Paste their URL or bio — LinkedIn URL, Instagram handle, website, or any text

What you get back
✦  Their communication personality type
✦  How to speak to them — and what to avoid
✦  What they care about most
✦  A suggested follow-up message — copy and send
Monthly Limit
Free accounts get 5 reads per month. Standard gets 5. Pro gets unlimited. Resets at the start of every month.
6
Protect your data

Your contacts live on your device. Export regularly — especially before clearing your browser or switching phones.

📊
Export to CSV
Settings → Export Contacts to CSV. Downloads a spreadsheet with all contacts, notes, events, and client tags. Open in Google Sheets or hand to your VA.
📱
Save to Phone
Open any contact → tap Save to Phone. Downloads a vCard with their photo. Opens in your phone's native contacts app — tap to save.
📄
PDF Contact Brief
Open any contact → tap PDF Brief. Professional one-pager with photo, notes, contact intelligence, and follow-up template. Hand to your VA.
Free: 3/month · Standard: 10/month · Pro: Unlimited
