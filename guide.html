Core Feature
Capturing contacts the right way
Two modes. One goal — never lose a connection again.
⚡ Quick Capture — Header Button
At a busy event and have 15 seconds? Tap Quick Capture at the top of the app — always visible. Just name and phone or email. Done in 10 seconds. Complete their profile later by tapping Edit.
⚡ Hot Intro — Dashboard Grid
Have a few minutes? Tap Hot Intro in Quick Actions. Fill in all their details. Upload their business card from Gallery and AI fills the form automatically.
📱 Gallery Tip
Take photos with your regular camera app first — the photo saves to your camera roll automatically. Then in FollowOp tap Upload from Gallery to scan it. Your photo is always safe even if you close the app mid-capture.
The Capture Workflow
1
Take the photo
Use your regular camera app to photograph their business card or screenshot their LinkedIn. Saves to your camera roll automatically.
2
Upload from Gallery
Open FollowOp → tap Hot Intro → Upload from Gallery → select the photo. AI reads it and fills the form with name, company, title, phone, email, and website.
3
Tag the event and client
Select the event from the dropdown — your logged events appear automatically. Select which client you're representing. Add your notes from the conversation.
4
Save and research later
Tap Save. Later — tap the contact and hit Research This Contact. AI searches the web for everything about them and saves the intelligence to their profile.
New Feature
Research This Contact —
know before you call
Before you follow up with anyone — research them. Walk into every conversation already knowing who they are, what they care about, and exactly what to say.
What it finds
✦  Online presence — LinkedIn, social media, website
✦  Business intel — Google reviews, company size, location
✦  Community affiliations — chambers, groups, events they attend
✦  Recent activity — news mentions, what they post about
✦  How to serve them better — specific follow-up approach
✦  3 conversation starters — specific things to reference
Three ways to research
👤
From Contact
Uses their saved name, company, and website automatically. Tap the contact → Research This Contact → From Contact → Research.
📋
Paste Info — Most Accurate
Paste their LinkedIn URL, Instagram handle, website, or any bio text. A LinkedIn URL alone gives full professional history, recent posts, and community involvement.
🖼
Gallery Upload
Screenshot their LinkedIn, Google Business page, or any profile. Upload from Gallery and AI reads everything visible in the image.
